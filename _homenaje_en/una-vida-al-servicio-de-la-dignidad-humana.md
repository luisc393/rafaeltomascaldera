Rafael Tomás Caldera Pietri was born in Caracas in 1945. He grew up in surroundings where culture, faith and thought were a natural part of life. From early on he showed a deep vocation: to understand the human being through philosophy and to teach others to do the same.

He graduated in law *magna cum laude* from the Universidad Central de Venezuela, holds a Master of Arts from the University of Notre Dame and a doctorate in Letters and Philosophy, with honours, from the University of Fribourg (Switzerland). His thesis, on judgement by inclination in Saint Thomas Aquinas, was published by the prestigious Paris publisher Vrin.

While studying law at the UCV he edited the magazine *Vértice*; he has also translated and edited numerous works and publications.

For almost three decades he taught at Universidad Simón Bolívar, devoted to forming whole generations in a love of reading, clarity of thought and the riches of the human spirit.

In his classrooms and in his writings he has taught that thinking is an act of freedom; that understanding is a form of loving; and that education is not limited to passing on knowledge, but is meant to awaken moral intelligence.

He is a full member of the Venezuelan Academy of Language, in chair Q; a member of the Pontifical Academy of Saint Thomas Aquinas since 1999; and an active member of the Venezuelan Philosophical Society. He also teaches at Universidad Monteávila.

In his writings and in his personal example he upholds the idea of a civilization of love, in which thought is at the service of human dignity.
