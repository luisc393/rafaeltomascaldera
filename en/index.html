Rafael Tomás Caldera (Caracas, 1945) is a Venezuelan philosopher, essayist and university professor. He has taught generations of students at Universidad Simón Bolívar and Universidad Monteávila, and is the author of more than twenty books on knowledge, love, education and Latin American culture.
